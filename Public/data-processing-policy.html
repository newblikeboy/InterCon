1. Parties, scope and acceptance

These Data Processing Terms govern InterCon's processing of customer and patient information on behalf of a business client. The provider is Synqvest System LLP. An authorised account owner accepts these terms for the client; we retain a record of that acceptance. A separately executed agreement or mandatory legal requirement takes precedence where applicable.

2. Instructions and responsibilities

The client determines the purpose and intended recipients and is normally the Data Fiduciary for its customer or patient information. InterCon acts as Data Processor for those instructions. InterCon separately determines processing for its account administration, billing and security. Roles depend on the actual activity.

The client must provide lawful instructions, issue the necessary notices and obtain and retain evidence of the required permissions. A declaration made through InterCon does not replace the individual's consent. InterCon does not obtain clinical consent from patients or independently establish their identity, age, guardianship or clinical relationship on the client's behalf.

3. Data, people and operations

Information may include recipient contact details, permission records, messages, attachments including patient-report PDFs, delivery information and necessary account or service identifiers. Individuals may include customers, patients, authorised guardians and business staff.

Processing includes instructed import, storage, delivery, display to authorised users, access logging, export, restriction and deletion. Use is limited to providing the agreed service and meeting applicable security and legal obligations. Client customer and patient information is not sold or used for InterCon's own advertising, cross-client marketing or model training.

4. Healthcare responsibilities

Before using InterCon for patient reports, the client must accept these terms and confirm that the intended disclosure is permitted by applicable healthcare, confidentiality and provider requirements. The client verifies the recipient's number, identity and authority, obtains the necessary permission for WhatsApp delivery and retains its supporting evidence.

Reports must be supplied through the designated confidential-report workflow and associated with the intended patient or authorised recipient. Actual patient information must not be used in general marketing assets or template-approval samples. Permission for report delivery does not authorise unrelated marketing.

InterCon restricts classified report delivery to the associated recipient. The client remains responsible for the medical content and accuracy and for maintaining any required clinical archive. Default report access for download and new sends ends after 30 days; storage and deletion are governed separately by retention requirements. Copies already delivered or downloaded cannot be recalled by InterCon.

5. Confidentiality and safeguards

InterCon maintains technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the instructed processing, including encryption, access restrictions, separation of client information and security monitoring. Access must be limited to authorised service, support, security and legal purposes and subject to applicable confidentiality duties.

Clients control their staff access and credentials, revoke access when no longer needed and report suspected compromise promptly. InterCon may restrict unsafe processing or suspend unlawful instructions while seeking clarification.

6. Providers and international handling

Service providers include DigitalOcean, MongoDB Atlas, Cloudinary, Meta/WhatsApp, Razorpay and the configured email provider. Each handles information relevant to its function; patient-report contents must not be included in billing or account-support communications. The client authorises the providers needed for the agreed service, subject to the applicable service and data-protection arrangements.

Provider locations, confidentiality, incident assistance and deletion arrangements must support the agreed processing. Standard service does not promise India-only storage. A client's stricter localisation or healthcare requirements must be expressly agreed before processing begins. InterCon provides notice of material provider changes where required by law or the applicable agreement.

7. Requests, withdrawal and return or deletion

The client handles its customers' identity checks and decisions on privacy requests. InterCon forwards direct requests to the responsible client and provides reasonable assistance with verified instructions, including access, correction, export, withdrawal and erasure. Required retention or a justified legal hold may defer deletion; it does not authorise unrelated use or marketing.

At termination or on a valid instruction, InterCon assists with return or deletion of instructed live information and applicable provider deletion. Limited withdrawal, security, payment and legal records may remain for justified periods. Backup copies are subject to their retention schedules and access restrictions; erasure decisions must be reapplied before restored information is put back into ordinary use. Recipient-held or independently stored client copies are outside InterCon's control.

8. Incidents and assistance

InterCon informs affected clients promptly after becoming aware of a relevant personal-data incident and provides available information about the scope, impact, mitigation and subsequent developments. The client assesses notices it must give to affected individuals and authorities. InterCon assesses its own obligations and provides reasonable cooperation. Applicable CERT-In and DPDP notification requirements are assessed separately.

9. Retention and audit cooperation

The client communicates its retention instructions and justified legal holds. Default retention is 400 days for security audit records and 366 days for message-delivery event records. Recipient-specific file download and new-send access expires after 30 days; this is not a physical-deletion deadline. Underlying files and other client information remain subject to the instructed purpose, deletion handling and applicable minimum retention. The client maintains any clinical archive required by law.

InterCon provides available evidence of safeguards and reasonably cooperates with proportionate reviews, subject to confidentiality and other clients' security. These terms do not represent ISO, HIPAA, DPDP or other certification. Mandatory law and its applicable commencement dates govern the processing.
