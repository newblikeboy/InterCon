Common questions

Is WhatsApp Business setup difficult?

It can feel technical — business details, number readiness, templates, policies, API configuration, and integrations. InterCon simplifies this into a guided checklist and handles the technical setup with you.

Can you help if I do not have a technical team?

Yes. We support non-technical businesses with onboarding, inbox setup, templates, and training so your sales or support team can use the system comfortably.

What can I use WhatsApp for?

Customer support, order updates, reminders, payment alerts, feedback, and approved bulk templates. External systems can send templates through the API. Automated scheduling and chatbot flows are planned.

Do you support RCS and SMS?

WhatsApp is live today. RCS Business Messaging and SMS are coming soon — join the waitlist and we'll onboard you as each channel launches, all from the same InterCon workspace and one API.
