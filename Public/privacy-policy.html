1. Who we are and our role

InterCon is a business messaging service operated by Synqvest System LLP. We process account, billing, support and security information to operate our service. For customer or patient information supplied by a business client, we process data on that client's documented instructions. The client determines the purpose and intended recipients and is responsible for the necessary notices and permissions.

We do not sell client customer or patient data or use clients' contact lists, reports or message contents for our own advertising, cross-client marketing or model training. Our Data Processing Terms explain the client and processor responsibilities. Reading this policy or browsing our website does not itself grant permission for unrelated marketing.

2. Information we handle

  • Account information: business and contact names, email, phone, login and verification information, subscription details and acceptance of service terms.
  • Client-supplied information: recipient names and numbers, other contact details, permission records, messages, attachments, templates, conversation history and delivery information.
  • Health information, where permitted: patient-report PDFs and related recipient and permission information supplied by a healthcare client. These documents may contain medical results and other sensitive information.
  • Service and security information: account and request identifiers, IP/network information, device/browser information, timestamps and access or security events.
  • Billing information: plan, payment references and transaction status. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider.

Clients should supply only information necessary for the requested service. Do not include patient reports, passwords, OTPs, payment-card details or identity-document scans in support requests.

3. Purposes and processing basis

We use account information to provide and administer InterCon, verify accounts, manage subscriptions, respond to support requests, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations. Client-supplied information is used for the messaging, storage and related functions instructed by the client.

Where consent is required, the responsible business must provide the relevant notice and obtain it. A client's declaration that it has permission is not independent verification by InterCon. The client retains the underlying evidence. Permission for service communications does not automatically authorise unrelated marketing.

4. Messages, attachments and recipients

Messages and attachments are made available to the selected recipients and the service providers involved in storage and delivery. Clients must have authority to share the information through the chosen channel and select the correct recipient. Healthcare use is subject to applicable confidentiality requirements and the additional responsibilities in our Data Processing Terms.

An incoming enquiry does not automatically grant permission for future business-initiated marketing. We honour supported STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE and opt-out instructions; clients must also record withdrawals received through other channels.

InterCon cannot control a shared recipient device, forwarded attachment or independently downloaded copy. Removing information from InterCon does not recall a message or attachment already delivered.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Our providers include Meta/WhatsApp for messaging, MongoDB Atlas for database hosting, DigitalOcean for application infrastructure, Cloudinary for media storage and delivery, Razorpay for payments, and the configured email provider for account notifications. The information shared depends on each provider's function. Payment and account-email providers do not need patient-report contents for those functions.

Providers handle information under the applicable service and data-protection arrangements. Some, including WhatsApp and payment providers, may also process information under their own terms and notices. We may disclose information when legally required or as necessary to investigate security incidents, prevent abuse or protect legal rights.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain active client information for the instructed service and applicable retention requirements. When the purpose ends, an account closes or we receive a verified deletion or withdrawal request, we review the information for deletion or restricted retention.

  • Recipient-specific file download and new-send access expires after 30 days by default. This does not mean the underlying file or an already delivered copy has been deleted.
  • The default retention periods are 400 days for security audit records and 366 days for message-delivery event records.
  • Report files and other client information may remain after access ends while deletion instructions, required minimum retention and documented legal holds are addressed. InterCon is not a laboratory's authoritative clinical archive.
  • Billing, incident evidence and other legally required records may need longer retention. Backup copies have separate retention and deletion schedules; deletion from the active service does not immediately remove every backup.

Applicable legal minimum periods may limit immediate deletion. Where retention is required, we explain the reason and restrict further use to the justified purpose. The client remains responsible for any medical-record retention obligations that apply to it.

7. Your rights and requests

You can request access, correction, erasure, withdrawal of consent, nomination or grievance handling, as applicable, through the Privacy Center or support@intercon.in. Include your account or business identifier and a brief description. We verify identity and authority before acting; do not send medical attachments or unnecessary identity documents.

If your information was supplied by a laboratory or another business client, contact that business first. It decides how your information is used, and InterCon assists with its verified instructions. We may retain limited records needed to honour a withdrawal, prevent further unwanted contact, maintain security or comply with legal obligations.

We aim to acknowledge requests within seven days and respond within 30 days, or sooner where applicable law requires. We explain any need for clarification or justified retention. Unresolved concerns may be raised with the appropriate authority through the legally available process.

8. Security and incidents

We use technical and organisational safeguards, including encryption, restricted access, separation of client information and security monitoring, to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, alteration, loss or disclosure. Authorised users and providers may access information as needed for the instructed service, support, security or legal obligations. Clients are responsible for protecting their accounts and controlling their staff access.

If we become aware of a relevant personal-data incident, we take steps to contain it, assess its impact, inform affected clients and meet applicable notification requirements. Report suspected incidents promptly to support@intercon.in.

No service can guarantee absolute security. This policy does not represent an independent security or compliance certification.

9. Client responsibilities

Clients must have authority to supply information, provide the necessary notices, obtain and retain required permissions, choose authorised recipients, honour withdrawals and communicate applicable retention instructions. Clients must not disclose another person's confidential information to an unauthorised recipient or use information for an incompatible purpose.

Additional responsibilities apply to health information and children's data. Permission for medical-report delivery does not automatically authorise marketing. The Data Processing Terms set out the relevant client and InterCon obligations.

10. Cookies and integrations

We use necessary cookies and similar information to maintain sessions, secure accounts and operate the service. Meta signup and payment integrations may use their own cookies under their respective notices. Optional tracking requires an appropriate notice and permission mechanism where required; this policy alone is not consent to it.

11. Children and authorised guardians

InterCon business accounts are intended for authorised adults. A client supplying a child's information must establish its authority and obtain any required verifiable parental or guardian permission, or establish a valid applicable exemption. Children's information must be limited to the permitted purpose and must not be used for prohibited tracking or targeted advertising.

12. International processing

Information may be processed in India or in countries where our providers operate, depending on the service and deployment. Standard InterCon service does not promise India-only storage. Client-specific localisation or healthcare requirements must be agreed before the relevant information is supplied. We address applicable transfer restrictions and any additional permissions required for the processing.

13. Changes and applicable law

Version 2026-10-29, effective upon publication. We publish material changes here and use account communications where appropriate. We obtain additional consent or contract acceptance where required.

The DPDP framework has phased commencement, with most operational obligations scheduled for May 2027 under the official commencement notification. Applicable existing law continues to apply during the transition. This policy does not certify that every client or use of the service complies with all applicable requirements.
